# Fluxo da amostra (CONSORT)

&nbsp;

## Participants

Eighty-three children and adolescents with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) were contacted; 45 were excluded (not meeting the inclusion criteria, n \= 4; declined to participate, n \= 37; did not understand the activity, n \= 4), leaving 38 participants with ASD. A control group of 38 participants without ASD was recruited, matched 1:1 to the ASD group by sex and age. Participants with ASD were characterised with the CARS and questionnaires before the game sessions.

The intervention consisted of OpenHeal game sessions of three matches each (M1–M3). On day 1 both groups played (ASD: n \= 38, 112 matches; control: n \= 38, 114 matches). A second session (day 2\) was held for the ASD group only, by study design; 36 of the 38 participants returned (107 matches). Three matches were excluded because of hand-tracking capture failure, so two ASD participants on day 1 and one on day 2 contributed two of the three matches. All participants were retained in the analysis: ASD, n \= 38 on day 1 and n \= 36 on day 2; control, n \= 38 on day 1 (Figure 1).
